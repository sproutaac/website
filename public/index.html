Why Sprout exists

Most AAC apps cost $250.
We think that's wrong.

Communication is a human right, not a luxury. Yet the best AAC apps lock families behind expensive subscriptions and fragile Wi-Fi connections. Sprout is free to download, free to use, and designed to work anywhere — including waiting rooms, cars, and places without signal. Built in the open, with feedback from the AAC community welcome. Built for the families who need it most.

How it works

Designed to grow
with your child.

Works anywhere, offline

Sprout keeps everything on the device, so once a board is set up it works without Wi-Fi: the doctor's office, the car, the playground.

Grows as they do

Start with a 3×3 grid of 9 essential words, then move up to 4×4 and 5×5 as your child develops. Same app, same familiar positions, no motor memory reset. Bigger grids are on the roadmap.

Fully customizable

Add symbols from a library of thousands. Change labels, colors, and layouts. Your child's board, your way. Importing boards from your SLP is coming in v0.2.

Private by design

Your child's data never leaves their device without your permission. No accounts required to use the app. No behavioral data collected.

Read the privacy policy →

Multiple access methods

Designed for touch, with switch scanning and eye gaze support in development. Every child's access needs are valid — we're building for all of them.

SLP collaboration

Coming in v0.2: therapists will be able to share boards directly with families, in the industry-standard OBF format so boards work across apps and devices.

For parents & caregivers

"I just want something that works for my child."

You shouldn't need a tech degree or a large budget to give your child a voice. Sprout is set up in under 5 minutes, free forever, and designed to feel natural for your whole family.

  • Ready-to-use starter boards based on your child's age
  • Add favorite words and symbols in seconds
  • PIN-protected editing so the board stays stable during use
  • Multiple child profiles on one device

For speech-language pathologists

"I need something I can confidently recommend."

Sprout uses industry-standard core vocabulary, Fitzgerald Key color coding, and ARASAAC imagery. It speaks the same language your practice does.

  • OBF import/export, compatible with other AAC systems (coming in v0.2)
  • Push boards directly to families without them paying a cent (coming in v0.2)
  • Motor planning stability: cells never move on the child
  • Open source — transparent, auditable, trustworthy

Accessible by design

Built so every child
can use it.

AAC should reach every child — not just those whose families can navigate complex setup. Sprout is designed with the widest possible range of access methods from the start.

Switch scanning

Single and dual-switch scanning is on the roadmap for children who can't use direct touch — visual scanning is already built, physical switch input is coming next.

Eye gaze

Eye tracking support is on the roadmap — compatible with affordable eye gaze hardware already in many clinics.

High contrast

Optimized for children with low vision. Toggle between standard and high contrast layouts instantly.

OpenDyslexic font

Switch to OpenDyslexic across the whole app in one tap — no settings buried in submenus.

Adjustable grids

From 3×3 for beginners to larger grids for advanced communicators. Start small and grow at your child's pace.

Works offline

Once a board is set up, no internet is needed. Your child's voice doesn't depend on Wi-Fi, a data plan, or a server staying online.

Privacy Policy

Your child's data
stays on their device.

We wrote this to be read, not to protect us from lawyers. If something isn't clear, email us and we'll explain it in plain English.

Last updated: October 2026

Stored on-device
All board data, profiles, and settings live on the device. We can't see them. Nobody can.
No account needed
You don't need to sign up to use Sprout. No account means nothing to collect.
Never sold
We will never sell, share, or monetize any information about you or your child. Full stop.

The app

Sprout stores everything — your child's boards, symbols, profiles, and settings — locally on your device. This data never leaves the device unless you explicitly export a board yourself (for example, to share with a therapist).

We don't collect:

  • Any information about your child — name, age, diagnosis, communication patterns
  • Usage analytics tied to your child or their communication
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or anything similar
  • Location data

Symbol pictures come from ARASAAC. The first time a symbol is shown, the app downloads its image from ARASAAC's servers (which see a normal web request, like any website visit), then keeps a copy on the device so it works offline after that. No information about your child is sent.

When you export a board to share with a therapist or another device, that data goes directly from your device to wherever you send it — it doesn't pass through our servers.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address, the date you signed up, and whether you ticked the beta-tester box. Here's exactly what we do with it:

  • Send a confirmation email right after you sign up
  • Tell you when Sprout is available to download, and share occasional development updates (never more than once a month)
  • Remove you immediately if you unsubscribe — every email has a one-click link, or just email us

Your email is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database and sent through Resend, our email provider. To stop the form being abused, we briefly keep a scrambled (hashed) form of the IP address that submitted it; we never store the address itself, and those records are deleted within an hour.

We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with anyone. We use it for one purpose: keeping you informed about Sprout. That's it.

The website

Sproutaac.org is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect standard server logs (IP addresses, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance purposes, and we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies or tracking you across sites. Fonts load from Google Fonts. We don't use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising trackers, or any other third-party tracking.

Children's privacy

Sprout is designed for use by children under the supervision of parents and caregivers. Because the app requires no account and stores no data on our servers, no personal information about children is ever transmitted to us. Sprout doesn't collect personal information from children, so there's nothing for COPPA's parental-consent rules to apply to. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy and ask parents first.

Open source transparency

Sprout is open source. You don't have to take our word for any of this — you can read the code and verify exactly what the app does and doesn't do. Our repository is publicly available on GitHub at github.com/sproutaac.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll notify waitlist subscribers by email and update the date at the top of this page. We won't bury changes in legalese — if something important changes, we'll say so plainly.
